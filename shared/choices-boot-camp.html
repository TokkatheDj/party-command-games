Choices

Boot Camp · a story where every choice counts

You ship out at 0400.

Ten weeks of basic training, then your first unit. You'll be told what to do all day, every day. Most of the time, doing what you're told is right. Not every time.

Every choice moves three things:

🎖️ Rank: how your leaders rate you.
🧭 Conscience: whether you did what was right.
🤝 Squad: whether the people beside you trust you.

Some choices come back later. Two strikes and you're sent home. There are seven endings.
