--- STEP 3: DESIGN GUARDRAILS ---
  - Target audience: general adults seeking self-help wellness support.
   - EMDR-inspired tools must be framed as relaxation and grounding aids only —
    not clinical EMDR therapy. Full EMDR trauma processing requires a licensed
    therapist. State this clearly in the app. Bilateral stimulation here is
    used solely for calm-state induction and stress reduction.
  - This is a SELF-HELP tool only — always include a gentle disclaimer:
    "This app is for general wellness support, not a substitute for
    professional mental health care. If you are in crisis, please contact
    a mental health professional or crisis line."
  - UI must feel calm and safe: soft colors (blues, greens, warm neutrals),
    rounded shapes, generous whitespace, no harsh reds or alarms.
  - Tone must be warm, gentle, and non-judgmental at all times —
    no shaming language, no pressure, no failure states.
  - If the tool logs data, use localStorage only — nothing leaves the device.
  - Include a clear "Close / Exit" and "Start Over" option always visible.
  - No ads, no external links, no data collection.
  - Do not attempt to diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinical care.

--- STEP 4: BUILD IT ---

Build the full app as one .html file. Requirements:
  - Entirely self-contained (no external files or CDN links that could break)

  - Wellness disclaimer visible on load or in a persistent footer
  - Genuinely complete — real exercises, real prompts, real logged data,
