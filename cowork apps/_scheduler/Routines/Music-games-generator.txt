SOUND KITS - build every sound from the kit's recipe (Web Audio, no files). Every sound has a
shaped envelope (attack 5-20 ms, exponential release - never a bare oscillator start/stop), one
master GainNode for the mute switch, and a DynamicsCompressor before the speakers. Give the app a
3-4 note signature motif (start/win), and make success, failure and "tap" clearly different.
A bare sine beep is not allowed in any kit.
  Plucked String - Karplus-Strong: a short noise burst into a feedback delay of 1/f with a lowpass
                   in the loop; pentatonic notes.
  Marimba        - FM: sine carrier + sine modulator at 4x frequency, modulation index falling fast;
                   200-1200 Hz, 0.4 s decay.
  Chiptune       - square and 25% pulse waves, quick arpeggios (3 notes in 60 ms), noise-channel
                   snare; strict 8-bit feel.
  Woodblock Kit  - bandpassed noise clicks (1.5-3 kHz, 30 ms), a pitched woodblock (triangle 800

  Toy Piano      - two detuned triangles + a short high "tine" partial, quick decay; slightly out of

  Cartoon FX     - pitch sweeps: "boing" (sine 200->600 Hz with vibrato), "pop" (noise + 900 Hz

  Music Box      - sine + 3rd harmonic with a bright 2-3 kHz partial, long 1.2 s decay, light

=== ACCESSIBILITY (required) ===
Applies to the app you build above. The nightly check scans every new app with axe-core; an app
that fails stays OFF the public Games Arcade until someone fixes it by hand. Before you save,
re-read your own CSS and markup against this list:
- CONTRAST (the commonest failure - it was 8 of 12 failing apps on 27 Oct): write the palette
  once at the top as CSS variables, and put text ONLY in these pairs - dark text (#1b1030) on any
  light or bright fill; white text only on fills at least as dark as #0977d0, #1e854a, #b0005a or
  #6b2fb3. Bright fills (green #2ec27e, orange #ff9f1c, yellow, cyan, pink) ALWAYS take dark
  text #1b1030 - white on them is about 2:1. Muted / hint / label / "meta" text on a dark panel
  is exactly #a9a3c0 (or lighter) - do not invent a darker grey or slate for "dim" text (29 Oct:
  #6f6283, #6d7899 and #6382a2 all failed) - and dark panels stay no lighter than #3a3550, where
  #a9a3c0 still reaches 4.8:1. Never fade text with opacity or an rgba alpha. A selected / "on" /
  active button or tab gets a DARK fill with white text, not a bright fill.
- EVERY CONTROL HAS A NAME: buttons have visible text or aria-label (emoji-only and icon-only
  buttons need aria-label); every <input>, <select> and <textarea> has a <label> or aria-label.
- SWITCHES AND TOGGLES: use a real <button aria-pressed>, or a role="switch" element that also has
  tabindex="0", aria-checked, aria-label, and flips on Space/Enter.
- KEYBOARD: everything clickable is a real <button> or <a> (not a clickable <div>/<span>).
- ROLES: only put role="list" / "listbox" / "grid" / "radiogroup" on something whose children
  have the matching roles - otherwise leave the role off.
