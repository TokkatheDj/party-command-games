Bizzy's Pollen Garden

Fly Bizzy the bee from flower to flower. Pollen from one flower makes another flower of the same kind grow a fruit!

How to play: tap a flower and Bizzy flies there to pick up its pollen.

Then tap a different flower of the same kind. Its petals fall and a fruit grows!

Grow every fruit in as few hops as you can. No rush, no losing.
