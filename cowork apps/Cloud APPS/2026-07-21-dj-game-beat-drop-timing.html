DROP IT

The track builds. A rising filter sweep, an accelerating kick, and a spinning platter all tell you the drop is coming. Press DROP (or hit spacebar) the instant the fill hits the glowing green zone. Too early or too late and the crowd loses energy. Nail it and the bass hits, the crowd cheers, and your combo grows. Each successful drop raises the level — faster builds, tighter windows.
