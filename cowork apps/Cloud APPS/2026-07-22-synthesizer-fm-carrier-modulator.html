VOLTAIC FM

2-operator FM synthesizer — play it live, sculpt the sound, save your patch
Producers / Musicians

A frequency-modulation synth voice: a Carrier oscillator gets its pitch warped by a Modulator oscillator to create everything from glassy bells to metallic plucks and deep FM basses. Shape it with a filter, an ADSR envelope that drives amplitude / filter / FM depth together, an LFO, and a delay + reverb send. Play it with the on-screen keys, your computer keyboard (A–; row, Z/X to change octave), or touch. Save patches to your browser's storage and reload them any time.
