Twist of Fate

Build one story together, a sentence at a time. Every line must flip the luck — and weave in the words you dared to stake.

ROUND-ROBIN STORYTELLING · 3–10 PLAYERS · 20–30 MIN · ONE SHARED SCREEN

Storytellers

Add them in seating order — the story travels around the circle.

How to play

  1. Each turn starts with FORTUNATELY… or UNFORTUNATELY… — your one sentence must swing the hero's luck that way.
  2. Draw 3 word cards and stake how many you'll weave in: 1 word = 1 pt, 2 = 3 pts, all 3 = 6 pts.
  3. Hit Go and speak before the timer ends. The room taps each word as they hear it.
  4. Miss one staked word, or forget to flip the luck? Zero. Twist cards add a +2 bonus challenge.
  5. The very last turn is the Final Chapter: wrap up the whole tale for double points.
