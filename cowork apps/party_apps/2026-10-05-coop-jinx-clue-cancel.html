How to play Jinx!

  1. You're all on one team. Try to save as many of the 13 cards as you can.
  2. Each card, one player is the Guesser (it rotates). Everyone else is a clue-giver.
  3. Pass the phone round the givers. Each one secretly sees the mystery word and types one single-word clue. No talking, no peeking at other clues.
  4. Then the jinx: any clues that match each other (including plurals and word forms like run/running) are cancelled. Givers can also tap to jinx clues that are basically the same.
  5. The Guesser sees only the clues that survive, then gets one guess. Right = card saved. Pass = card lost. Wrong = this card and the next are lost.
  6. Your clue can't contain the mystery word or be part of it. With 3 players, each giver writes two clues.
  7. The trick: the obvious clue is the one everyone else writes. Be helpful, but be different.
