Off By One

Argue five things into the right order.
Get one pair backwards and the room will pounce.

Teams
Ladders per team
  1. One team gets a ladder — five things and a scale. Tap two tiles to swap them until the order looks right, then Lock It In.
  2. Before the answer shows, any rival team may challenge one neighbouring pair they think is backwards.
  3. +1 per tile in the correct slot, +2 for a perfect ladder. A good challenge steals +2; a bad one hands the ladder team +1.
  4. The last ladder of the night is the Final Ladder — everything doubles.
