How to play

  • Take turns adding one letter to the shared fragment. In Either end mode, tap ◀ or ▶ to pick the side.
  • Every letter must keep a real word possible — but if you complete a word (of the minimum length or longer), you lose the round.
  • Stuck? Bluff with a letter anyway and hope nobody notices.
  • Challenge: think the last player bluffed? They must type a real word that fits. The room votes. Real word → challenger loses. Fake or no word → bluffer loses.
  • WORD!: think the last letter finished a word? The room votes. Real → they lose. Not a word → you lose.
  • Each lost round earns a letter of G-H-O-S-T. Spell it and you're a ghost. Last one living wins.
  • Haunting: ghosts can't talk to the living — but once per round each ghost may tap 👻 to ban a letter for the current player's turn.
  • The phone doesn't know every word — the room is the judge. The ghost's dictionary only gives hints.
