How it works

1. The phone draws an event and the performer picks a target: Safe, Bold or Long Shot. Harder targets win more but cost chips if missed.

2. Everyone else stakes 1–3 chips to BACK (they'll do it) or LAY (they won't), or passes. Every stake goes into one pot. The house adds a seed to each side.

3. The performer plays. The phone times it and judges it, so there's no arguing.

4. The winning side splits the whole pot by stake. If you're alone on the right side, you take it all. If nobody backed the winning side, the pot rolls into a jackpot for the next event.

Out of chips? You still get a free 1-chip “pity stake” each event. Most chips after the last lap wins.
