How to host

1. Read the question aloud. Both teams shout answers.
2. Tap the team that landed a real answer first — they take control.
3. Tap any tile the controlling team names to flip it. Tap STRIKE when they miss.
4. Three strikes and the rival team gets one steal guess for the whole pot.
5. Mis-tapped? UNDO takes it back.
Everything runs on one screen. Pass nothing, pinch nothing.
