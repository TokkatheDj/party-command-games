Clash

Six things on a card. You secretly pick which one the group will crown #1 and which they'll dump in last place. Points for reading the room — not for being right.
3–10 players  •  15–25 min  •  one device, passed around
How to play

1. A card appears with a category and six items. Somebody reads it out loud. Argue about it — that's half the fun.

2. Pass the device around. Each player secretly taps one ★ TOP (what the group will rank first) and one ▼ BOTTOM (what the group will rank last).

3. The screen tallies every vote and reveals the group's true ranking.

4. Scoring — ★ on the real #1 = 3 pts, on #2 = 1 pt. ▼ on the real last = 3 pts, on second-to-last = 1 pt. And if you were the only person to call it and you nailed it: +2 Lone Wolf bonus.

You're not guessing the truth. You're guessing your friends.
