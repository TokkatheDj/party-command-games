    How to play

    1. The Keeper secretly locks a word into the phone. Only its first letter is shown.
    2. Everyone else thinks of any word that starts with the revealed letters and gives a riddle clue out loud — without saying the word.
    3. If you think you know another player's word, shout “CONTACT!” and tap the big button. You and the clue-giver count down together and shout your words on zero.
    4. The Keeper is trying to stop you: if they can name the clue-giver's word before zero, they say it and tap BLOCK. That word is burnt.
    5. If the pair shouted the same word — Contact! The Keeper must reveal the next letter, and the new prefix applies from now on.
    6. Attackers win the round when every letter is revealed, or when they make contact on the secret word itself. The Keeper wins if the round clock runs out (or the attackers give up).
    Example. Showing PA. Mia: “Is it something you open when it rains… no wait, when you jump out of a plane?” Leo: “CONTACT!” — 3, 2, 1 — both: “PARACHUTE!” The Keeper's word was PARACHUTE — attackers win!
    Scoring. Each contact: +1 to both players involved. A winning contact: +3 each. Keeper: +1 per block, and if they survive: +2 plus 1 per letter still hidden.
