How to play

Each case has a Keeper who secretly reads the true story behind a strange scene. Everyone else asks yes/no questions out loud; the Keeper taps the answer so the whole room sees it.

Every case hides 3–5 key facts. Ask a question that uncovers one and the Keeper taps 🔑 Fact — you score it and it goes up on the board for everyone. ★ facts are the heart of the story.

Think you've got it? Shout SOLVE and tell the whole story. Right = 5 / 3 / 2 points (it drops each time a hint leaks). Wrong = −1 and you're frozen out for a minute. The Keeper earns +2 when their case gets solved, so answer clearly!

Already know a case? The Keeper can swap it before showing the room — no penalty.
