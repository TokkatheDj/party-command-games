    How to play

    👄 The Mouther

    Each turn, one team picks a Mouther. They hold to peek at a secret phrase, then mouth it to their own team with zero sound — no whispering, humming, clapping out syllables or pointing at things. Gestures for "sounds like" or word count are fine.

    🧑‍⚖️ The Judge

    The phone goes to the next team, who holds it where the guessers can't see. Hit START. Every time a guesser says a word from the phrase out loud, tap it. Order doesn't matter, and tiny words (the, a, of…) are free.

    Hear a sound from the Mouther? Tap SOUND. Each costs 1 point, and the third busts the whole turn to zero.

    🎴 Handicap cards

    After peeking, the Mouther may take the offered card (Teeth Together, Big Grin, Sky Gazer…). Breaking the card counts as a SOUND. Survive it and the turn scores double.

    ⭐ Scoring

    • +1 per word, +2 for long words (8+ letters)
    • 👯 Twin Lips deck: +1 extra per word (b/p/m and f/v look identical!)
    • +3 Clean Sweep for getting every word
    • +2 Speed bonus for sweeping with half the time left
    • ×2 with a handicap card, then −1 per sound

    Each team gets one 🔄 redraw per game. Most points after everyone's turns wins.
