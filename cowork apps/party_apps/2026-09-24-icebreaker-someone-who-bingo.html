Someone Who?

Secretly answer a few quick facts about yourself, then race to match the room's surprising facts to the right people on a shared bingo board. Every correct guess comes with a story.

How to play

  1. Secret round: pass the phone — everyone taps YES or NO to quick "someone who…" facts about themselves.
  2. The board: the app turns the room's rarest facts into a 4×4 bingo board.
  3. Your turn: pick a square and name someone it's true for — or call NOBODY ELSE. Right = you claim it and they tell the story. Wrong = the square shows who it isn't.
  4. Mingle Minute: chat and fish for clues — but fibbing is allowed!
  5. Points: 3 if only one person fits, 2 if two, 1 if more. +2 for finishing any line, +5 BINGO if the whole line is your colour.
