HOW TO PLAY
1

One team is up. That team picks a Guesser — rotate to a new person every turn. The Guesser turns around and shuts their eyes.

2

Everyone else on the team reads the secret scene and has a few seconds to arrange themselves into a frozen photograph of it. Bodies only — no talking, no sounds, no pointing at objects in the room.

3

The shutter fires. The prompt vanishes from the screen, so the phone is safe to hand straight to the Guesser. They open their eyes and get one guess while the pose is held.

4

Stuck? Tap UNFREEZE ONE — a single actor gets two seconds to move, then re-freezes. Costs 2 points.

5

Miss it and the pose stays frozen — rival teams get one shot to steal it for 2 points.

6

The phone then goes back to the actors for the next shot — the app will tell you when. Same guesser until the turn ends, so they keep turning away.

Scoring
★ scene = 3  ·  ★★ = 4  ·  ★★★ = 5
Unfreeze One: −2  ·  Steal: +2 to the rival
All shots in a turn correct: +3 perfect set
House rules
Statues may not speak, mouth words, or spell letters. Props are allowed but only what's already in the room. Wobbling is legal. Laughing is inevitable.
