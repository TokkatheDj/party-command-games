Wink Out

The classic wink-murder circle game with your phone as referee. A secret Villain knocks Guests out with a sly wink. Spot them before the room empties!

👥 4–16 players⏱ 15–30 min📱 one phone, face-up in the middle

In one breath

Everyone secretly peeks at a role. Then you all chat face to face while the Villain winks people out. Get winked at and you count, faint, and tap your name. The Inspector gets three accusations to catch the Villain before the clock runs out.

  1. Deal: pass the phone once. Each player holds the button to peek at their card, then hides it.
  2. Play: put the phone face-up in the middle. Read the Method card (how the Villain strikes) and the Faint Style card (how you go down).
  3. Got winked? Count silently, faint in style, then tap your own tile. Don't give away who did it!
  4. Inspector mode: the Inspector taps ACCUSE. Guilty = caught. Wrong = one strike.
  5. Mob mode: there's no Inspector. Anyone can accuse, but they need a seconder. If they're wrong, the accuser and the seconder both faint.
  6. Points for villain knockouts, catches and surviving. The Inspector role rotates every round.

Family-friendly: nobody gets hurt, they just faint, fall asleep or power down, depending on the theme.
