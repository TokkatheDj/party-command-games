Lock & Ladder

One word. Two teams. Change a letter, steal the ladder.

Teams

Rounds (even, so both teams start equally often)

Turn timer

How to play

  1. Both teams share one word. Each team has its own target word on screen.
  2. On your turn, tap a tile and change one letter to make a new real word. Everyone shouts ideas!
  3. Put your target letter in its slot and that tile LOCKS for you. Points for every lock.
  4. Careful: drop the rival's letter in its slot and you've gifted them the lock.
  5. Every word is used up once played. No going back.
  6. First team to 3 locks ends the round and gets +1 bonus. Two passes in a row (stuck or out of time) also ends it.
  7. Word not in the dictionary? The room can vote to allow it.
