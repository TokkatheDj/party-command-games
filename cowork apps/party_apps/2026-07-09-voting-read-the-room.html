🗳️ Read the Room

A "most likely to..." voting game. Everyone votes in secret —
score a point when you vote with the majority.

3–10 players · one shared phone/tablet · ~15–20 minutes

Rounds

How to play: a prompt appears → pass the device around → everyone secretly taps who fits best → the votes are revealed. Everyone who picked the crowd favourite scores +1.
