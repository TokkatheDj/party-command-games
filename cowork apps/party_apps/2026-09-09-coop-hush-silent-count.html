The Silent Count
◈ CO-OP — THE WHOLE ROOM VS THE APP
  1. The phone deals everyone a secret hand of numbers from 1–100. Memorise yours, then pass it on.
  2. Together you must play every card in the room in ascending order — lowest first, all the way up.
  3. No talking. No counting. No hints. No “mine's high”. Just breathe and feel it.
  4. When your gut says go, tap your own badge. Every card lower than yours burns and the group loses a shard.
  5. Clear the level, everyone gets one more card. Survive to the last level and the room wins together.
