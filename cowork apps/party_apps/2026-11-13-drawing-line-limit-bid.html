Line Limit

Bid down how few straight chalk lines your artist needs to draw a secret clue. Win the bid, draw it in that many lines, and your team must guess it before the clock runs out.

3–12 players in 2–4 teams · ~15–25 min · one shared screen, host taps the buttons

How a round works

1. Everyone sees the category. 2. Teams take turns bidding lower ("we can do it in 5!") or passing. 3. The lowest bidder's artist peeks, then draws: every drag is one straight line. No letters, numbers, talking or pointing. 4. Team guesses out loud. Miss it and the other teams can steal.
