A bluff & deception party game

STRAIGHT
FACE

The game secretly tells you whether your story is TRUE or a LIE. Tell it. Survive the cross-examination. See who the room believes.

3–10 players  ·  20–35 minutes  ·  one screen, passed around
Rounds per player

How to play

  1. One player takes the screen and holds down to peek at their secret card: a story prompt, plus a hidden verdict — TRUE STORY or MAKE IT UP.
  2. They tell the story out loud to the room. 45 seconds.
  3. CROSS-EXAMINATION. 45 seconds where anyone can fire questions at them. They must answer — instantly, confidently, in character.
  4. Everyone else votes in secret on the screen: TRUE or LIE.
  5. Reveal. Guess right: +100. Teller earns +150 for every person they fooled — and a +250 bonus for fooling the entire room.

Random TWISTS land on some rounds — no hands, one-word answers, double points. The screen tells you when.
