Fastest team clock wins.
2–4 teams  •  3–10 players  •  15–25 min

HOW TO PLAY

One sentence

Your team lines up, the device starts at player 1, and each player must finish the challenge on screen — their teammates decide when it's done — then tap PASS and hand the device on. The clock stops when the last player finishes.

The details
  • Teams take turns running a leg. One team at a time — everyone else watches and heckles.
  • Teammates are the judges. If they say it's done, it's done. Be generous, it's faster.
  • Stuck? Tap SKIP — new card, but a time penalty gets added.
  • Each round has a TWIST that changes penalties or hands out time bonuses.
  • Lowest total time after all rounds wins the Gauntlet.
