Twenty Down

20 questions. One shared pot. Every answer helps your rivals.

Players (3–10)

Secret decks

Game length

Twist cards

How to play

  • Each round one player is the Oracle and secretly sees a thing. Everyone else is racing to name it.
  • Take turns asking one yes/no question out loud. The Oracle answers and taps it in. Each answer drains the shared pot by 1. Everyone hears every answer.
  • On your turn, you can GUESS instead of asking. Get it right and you bank whatever is left in the pot. A miss costs 1 and passes the turn.
  • Can't wait? BLURT a guess any time, even out of turn. A correct blurt banks the pot. A wrong one costs −3 and benches you for the rest of the round.
  • At half pot the word lengths are revealed, and at a quarter the first letters. If the pot runs dry, it's Sudden Death: one guess each, worth 1 point.
  • The Oracle earns +2 when the secret is solved (+1 if it only falls in Sudden Death), so give clear answers!
