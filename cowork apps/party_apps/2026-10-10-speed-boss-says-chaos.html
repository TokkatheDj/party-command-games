Boss Says: Chaos Edition

The screen shouts silly commands — only obey the ones from the BOSS. Fall for a trap and you're out. Last one standing wins!

3–12 players · about 10–15 minutes · one screen, one host

How to play (10 seconds)

  1. BOSS SAYS … → do it, fast!
  2. A command with no Boss (or a fake boss like "Captain says") → DON'T move!
  3. Twists (Freeze! Silence!) and Races (last one to do it is out) pop up too.
  4. The host taps anyone who messed up, then presses NEXT. Survive each command = 1 point; winning = 5!
