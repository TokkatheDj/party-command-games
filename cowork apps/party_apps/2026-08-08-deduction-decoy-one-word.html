DECOY

Everyone sees the same secret word.
One of you doesn't.
How it works
  • Pass the phone — each player privately reveals their card.
  • Go around the room; each player says ONE word out loud that hints at the secret word.
  • Too obvious and you hand it to the Decoy. Too vague and you look like the Decoy.
  • Then everyone votes. Catch the fake — or let them escape.
3–12 players · one shared phone · 20–30 min
