How to play

  1. Lay the phone flat in the middle. Everyone presses and holds their own pad (laptop: hold your key). iPhones read at most 5 fingers at once, so use a tablet or turn the Heckler on for 6 players.
  2. Once all fingers are down, a 3-2-1 countdown runs and the screen goes black. No clock, no numbers.
  3. Lift your finger the moment you think the target time has passed.
  4. Within 0.25 s is a Bullseye (5 pts). Then 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 as you drift up to 3 s off, plus +1 for the closest.
  5. Heckler: count out loud wrongly, hum, clap off-beat, tell a story. No touching the phone or the players. +1 for every player who ends up more than 2 s off.
