GUT CLOCK

Speed & dexterity · 3–10 players · 12–20 min · one shared screen

How long is
a second?

Finger on your pad. The clock goes dark. Tap when your gut says the time is up — closest to the real second wins.

Round types

  • Blind Clock clock shows a few seconds, then goes dark.
  • Lying Clock a clock runs at a secret wrong speed — ignore it.
  • Echo watch the red block for a secret time, then copy it.
  • Tick Tock 4 ticks at a secret tempo — tap on tick #N.
  • Double Tap two targets, two taps.

Up to 4 play at once (keys A · F · J · L on a laptop). Lay a phone flat in the middle; heats rotate so everyone plays every round.
