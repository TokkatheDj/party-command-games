How to play

One phone, tablet or TV is the quiz screen. One person is the host: they tap the buttons and judge. Everyone else splits into teams and shouts answers.

  • Each puzzle is a string of emoji that spells out an answer: 🔥🐶 = Hot dog, 🧊🏝️ = Iceland, 🌧️🐱🐶 = Raining cats and dogs.
  • Puzzles start at 3 points. As the clock runs, letter blanks appear (2 pts), then first letters (1 pt).
  • When a team shouts, the host taps that team and marks it ✓ or ✗. A wrong shout locks the team out of that puzzle.
  • Round 1 · Open Buzz: any team can answer.
  • Round 2 · Hot Seat: one team at a time plays alone. If they miss or pass, the rivals can STEAL it for 2.
  • Final · Drip Feed: the emoji appear one at a time, and points are doubled. Guess early for up to 8 points.
  • The host can hold 👁 to secretly check the answer. Only the host should look.
