FORBIDDEN FIVE

Get your team to guess the word — without saying the 5 forbidden words!

4–10 players · 2 teams · ~20 minutes

How to play

  1. Split into two teams. One clue-giver holds the device; a rival player sits beside them to catch violations.
  2. Describe the big word. Say any forbidden word (or the word itself) and the rival taps TABOO! (−1).
  3. Each guess your team gets right: tap GOT IT (+1). Stuck? SKIP (free, but time burns).
  4. When the buzzer sounds, pass the device to the other team. First to the target score after equal turns wins!
