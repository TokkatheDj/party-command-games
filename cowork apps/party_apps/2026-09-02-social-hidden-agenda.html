Hidden Agenda

Everyone gets a secret mission. Then you all just… talk. Afterwards the room tries to work out who was up to what.

Who's playing
3 to 10 players
Setup
Talk time
How long the conversation runs each round
Rounds
New missions and a new topic each round
Clean Slate card
One player gets no mission at all — they score by looking guilty anyway. A decoy mission joins the lineup.
Curveballs
A rule change drops on the room halfway through the talk
How to playClose
  1. Briefing. Pass the phone around. Each player privately reads their secret mission — something to pull off during ordinary conversation, like "get somebody to stand up".
  2. The talk. An agenda item appears and the clock starts. Everyone discusses it out loud like normal humans, while quietly working their mission. Don't be obvious.
  3. The lineup. Time's up. Every mission in play is now shown to the room, shuffled and unlabelled. This is when it all clicks — that's why she kept mentioning her knees.
  4. Accusations. Pass the phone again. Each player secretly names one person and one mission letter: who was running what.
  5. Debrief. One by one, each mission is revealed. The room decides out loud whether that player actually pulled it off.
