How to play Fishbowl

  1. Fill the bowl. Pass the phone around. Each player secretly writes a few slips (people, characters, things, places, actions). Or tap Deal deck if you don't want to write.
  2. Take turns. Teams alternate. The clue-giver holds the phone and gets their team to guess as many slips as possible before the timer runs out. Tap GOT IT for each one. Only your own team may guess.
  3. Same slips, harder rules. When the bowl is empty the round ends and every slip goes back in.
    🗣️ Round 1: describe it with any words except the word itself.
    ☝️ Round 2: ONE word only.
    🎭 Round 3: act it out, no sounds.
    🔊 Optional Round 4: sounds only, hands behind your back.
  4. Bonus time. Empty the bowl with seconds to spare and your team keeps those seconds to start the next round.
  5. Fouls. Break the round's rule? Tap Foul: the slip goes back and you lose 5 seconds. Skips are limited too, so choose them carefully.

1 point per slip. Most points after the last round wins.
