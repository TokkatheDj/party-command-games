LIVE WIRE
RELAY

Slide the ring along the electric wire without touching the edges, then pass the phone down your team's line.

2–4 teams · 4–16 players · ~15–25 min · one shared phone, tablet or laptop

HOW TO PLAY

Grab the glowing ring, drag it to END without touching the edges, then hand the device to your next runner. The clock never stops, and the fastest team wins.

Touch an edge: ZAP, +3s and back to the last checkpoint. Lift your finger: +1s. A leg with no slips: −1s. Every team runs the same course each round.
