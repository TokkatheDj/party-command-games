Rule cards bend roughly half the rounds — word-ladder chaining, 5-letter words only, double letters, alphabet climbs.

How to play
  • One Chainer per team holds the phone. Teammates shout ideas — that's allowed and encouraged.
  • Type one word at a time. Each word must connect to the one above it somehow.
  • Type the target word to close the chain. Beat par for bonus points.
  • Then rivals may challenge one junction. The room votes: fair link, or nonsense? Bogus links cost you.
