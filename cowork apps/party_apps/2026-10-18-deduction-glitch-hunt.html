Glitch Hunt

Every robot in the room shares one secret glitch. The Technician asks questions until they can name it before the clock runs out.

👥 3–12 players · ⏱ 15–30 min · one phone or laptop

👥 Players

Sit in a circle. Some glitches use “the person on your left”, so seats matter.

⚙️ Settings

Interview time

🦠 Virus mode

Virus mode: the phone is passed round the crew and one robot secretly gets a different glitch. Needs 5+ players.

📋 How to play

  1. The Technician leaves the room. Everyone else reads the secret glitch on the phone.
  2. The Technician comes back and asks anyone anything. Every robot answers while obeying the glitch.
  3. If a robot slips, the crew shouts REBOOT! and everyone swaps seats. The Technician gets a bonus point.
  4. The Technician says a diagnosis out loud and the crew rules on it. 3 misses or the buzzer sends them to the Diagnostic Lineup: pick the real glitch from 6.
