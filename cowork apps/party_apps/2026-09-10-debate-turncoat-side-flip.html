How it runs

One debater stands up. The screen gives them a motion and a side. They argue it out loud — badly, loudly, whatever works.

At random moments a klaxon fires and the screen flips to the opposite side. They must keep talking and start arguing against everything they just said. No pause, no restart.

The room watches. Tap STUMBLE when they freeze or contradict themselves, and throw one CURVEBALL word they have to work in.
