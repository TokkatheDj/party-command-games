Hum Dinger

The humming party game. One Hummer, one secret song, no words allowed. Can your team name that tune before the points drain away?

👥 4–16 players in 2–4 teams · ⏱ 20–30 min · one shared phone or laptop

📜 How to play

  1. Each turn, one player is the Hummer. They peek at a secret song on the phone, then hide it.
  2. They hum it: no words, no lyrics, no whistling. Clapping the beat is fine.
  3. Only their own team guesses. The host taps GOT IT the moment someone names the title.
  4. Too slow? Every rival team gets one shout to steal it.
  5. The team with the most points after the last round wins.
