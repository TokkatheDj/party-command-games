UNSIGNED.

Who wrote it? · 3–10 players · ~15–25 min

Un­signed.

Everyone secretly writes a true answer about themselves. Then you guess who wrote what. Be honest, but not too obvious: you only score as a writer if some of your friends spot you, not all of them.

Players3–10
Time~15–25 min
NeedsOne phone or laptop

Players

Rounds

How a round works

  1. A prompt appears. Pass the phone: everyone secretly types a true answer about themselves.
  2. The answers show up unsigned. Read them all out loud.
  3. Pass the phone again: each player secretly matches every answer to its writer. +1 for each correct match.
  4. Writers get +2 if SOME players spotted them but NOT ALL. Spotted by nobody or by everybody scores 0.

The person holding the phone runs it. Everyone else watches the big reveals.

Used prompts are remembered on this device, so the next game gets fresh ones.
