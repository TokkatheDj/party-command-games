RINGLEADER

One secret Ringleader. Everyone copies. One Spotter in the middle tries to catch who's really leading — before the next chime.

👥 4–12 players⏱ 15–25 min🕺 Get up & move
How to play
  1. Stand in a circle. The Spotter turns away while the phone secretly shows everyone else the Ringleader.
  2. Ringleader makes a move — everyone copies it. Put the phone on the floor in the middle.
  3. On every 🔔 chime, the Ringleader must switch to a new move.
  4. Spotter taps ACCUSE any time — 3 guesses. Catch them fast for big points; Ringleaders score for every chime they survive.
