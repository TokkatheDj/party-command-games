ALIBI

Two of you are accused. You get 25 seconds to get your story straight — then you're questioned separately.

  • The room picks which questions get asked.
  • Each suspect answers on the phone alone, no peeking.
  • Answers that match score you both. Answers that don't hand the points to the interrogators.
  • One question every round is worth double.
3–12 players · one shared phone · 20–30 min
