Match Snatch

Every two cards share exactly one picture. Spot the one your card shares with the middle card, tap it first and snatch the round!

2–10 players · up to 4 at the table each round · ~10–20 min · lay the phone flat between you

How to play

  1. Sit around the phone or tablet. Each player owns one card in their own corner.
  2. Find the ONE picture your card shares with the big middle card and tap it on your card.
  3. Wrong tap? Your card freezes for 1.5 seconds.
  4. Each round deals a new mode and twist. Winner gets 3 points, runner-up 1. Final round counts double.

Players

Tap the type button to switch a seat between Human and Bot (Easy · Medium · Hard). On a laptop with one mouse, play against bots.
