Slow Focus

A mystery picture starts as a blurry, zoomed-in smudge and slowly comes into focus. Buzz when you think you know it. The sooner you buzz, the more points.

3–12 players in 2–4 teams · about 15–20 min · one shared screen

One-line rules: slam your team's pad, then pick the answer from 4 choices. Right scores the points left on the meter. Wrong locks your team out for that picture, and every other team has now seen the choices.

The host (whoever is nearest the screen) taps "Reveal picture" each time. On a laptop, teams can buzz with keys 1–4.
