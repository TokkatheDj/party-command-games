Vowel Play

PPP RN
The team quiz where every answer has lost its vowels.
Buzz first, shout it out, steal what your rivals fumble.
4-16 players in 2-4 teams · one shared screen · ~15-30 min

How to play

One person is the Host: they hold the screen where everyone can see it and judge answers. The Host can play too, or just run the show.

Every clue is a real answer from the category with all its vowels (A E I O U) removed. Y stays. So BLD GL in “Birds” is BALD EAGLE.

Slam your team's buzz pad first, then shout the full answer. The Host taps Right or Wrong. A wrong answer costs a point and locks your team out of that clue, so rivals can steal it.

Round 1 · Warm Up (1 pt): spaces are in the right places.
Round 2 · Scrambled (2 pts): the spaces have been shuffled, so PP PRN could be PEPPERONI.
Round 3 · Drip Feed (3→1 pts): vowels drip back one by one. Buzz early for 3 points, or wait for help and win less.
Round 4 · Mystery Category (2 pts): scrambled, and the category is hidden until someone gets the first answer.

Final Wager: each team bets some of its points on one long saying. Teams whisper or write their answer for the Host, then everything is revealed at once.

Host tools: Peek (hold to see the answer), Reveal (nobody scores), Undo. Keyboard: 1-4 buzz, Y/N judge, Space next.
