CLUE LADDER

Trivia duels where every clue is easier than the last, and worth less. Guess early for big points, or wait and risk your rival grabbing it first.

3–10 PLAYERS · ~15–25 MIN · ONE SHARED SCREEN

HOW TO PLAY

  • Two players duel. A secret answer hides behind 5 clues, hardest first.
  • On your move: GUESS out loud (5 pts on rung 1, down to 1 on rung 5) or PASS, which shows the next clue to your rival.
  • Guess wrong and your rival gets the next clue and one free shot.
  • A Referee (someone not duelling) peeks at the answer and taps right or wrong.
  • Win a 3-card bout to keep the 👑 crown (+2). The loser goes to the back of the line. Highest total at the end wins.

PLAYERS

GAME LENGTH

A lap means every player gets one turn as a challenger. The last duel is worth double.

Keyboard: G = guess · P = pass · Y / N = referee right / wrong · Enter = next
