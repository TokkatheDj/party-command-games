How it works

1. Two contenders are called out and letters are drawn — say, P.B.J.
2. The phone goes to each of them in turn. Privately, they type what it stands for, fitting the brief.
3. Both answers appear anonymously on the shared screen. The room votes out loud and whoever's holding the phone taps the winner.
4. The winner keeps the belt and defends it next round. Three straight wins starts stacking streak bonuses.
