Tongue Tied

Say a tongue twister one word per beat. Every lap the beat gets faster, and you keep going until you flub. The room is the judge.

2–10 players · ~15–25 min · one phone

How to play

On your turn, pick a Warm-up (×1), Tricky (×2) or Brutal (×3) twister and prop the phone where you can read it. Say each word as it lights up, on the beat. After every lap the tempo jumps. Anyone who hears a slip slams FLUB. You score laps × difficulty. Feeling brave? Call your shot before you start: hit it for a big bonus, miss it and you score nothing.
