Common Ground

3–10 players · 15–25 min · one shared screen

Pairs get 75 seconds to talk and find what they really have in common. Then the room fact-checks them: back to back, both answer at once. Fibs bust!

How a turn works

  1. A pair (or a trio) faces 8 tiles like “same birth month”. Chat out loud, and tap every one you truly share: 1, 2 or 3 points.
  2. Everyone else is a Fact-Checker and picks 2–3 claimed tiles to audit.
  3. Back to back, on 3, the pair both say the answer. A match doubles that tile. A bust loses it, halves the turn, and gives every Fact-Checker +1.

Everyone plays with a new partner each round. Highest total wins.
