AlphabetScene

Act it out, A to Z!

Two players act out a silly scene where every line must start with the next letter of the alphabet. The room referees. Get as far as you can before 3 fumbles or the clock runs out.

3–12 players~15–25 minOne screen, room referees

Who's playing?

    Add at least 3 players.

    Game options

    Laps (everyone performs once per lap)
    Scene clock
    Stall bar (seconds per line)
    Twist scenes

    How a scene works

    1. The screen deals a scene and a random start letter, say R.
    2. Players take turns speaking: "Right, where's my spoon?" then "Seriously? You ate it." then "Totally not."…
    3. The room taps NEXT after each good line, BUZZ for a wrong letter or a fumble. If the stall bar runs out, that's a strike too.
    4. Every letter cleared scores both actors. J K Q V X Z score 2. Make it all 26 for +5!

    Keyboard: Space = Next, B = Buzz, F = Freebie skip.
