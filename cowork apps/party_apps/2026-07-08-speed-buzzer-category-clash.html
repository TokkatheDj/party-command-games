CATEGORY CLASH

A category flashes — SLAM your buzzer first, then shout an answer before the countdown hits zero!

3–8 players (2–4 buzz at a time, everyone else judges & rotates in) · ~15 minutes

  1. When the screen says GET READY… hands off! Buzzing early locks you out for that round.
  2. Category appears → first to tap their color (or press their key) gets 5 seconds to shout an answer.
  3. The group judges: Good = +1. Blank or bogus = −1, and everyone else can steal next round.
  4. First to the target score wins. Swap seats between games so everyone buzzes!
