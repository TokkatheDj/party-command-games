HOW TO PLAY

One phone, propped where everyone can see it. Everybody plays at once.

1. The Fence posts an order — “something older than everyone here”.

2. Everyone scatters and grabs one real object from the room before the clock hits zero, comes back and taps their own name to check in. Your check-in position is recorded.

3. Only then does the Fence say which of the six rules actually paid. That is the whole game: you are betting on a rule you cannot see.

House rules: nothing sharp, hot, breakable, or belonging to someone who isn’t playing. Stay inside. Put it all back afterwards.
