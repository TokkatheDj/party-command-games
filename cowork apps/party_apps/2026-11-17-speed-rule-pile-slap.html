Rule Pile

Cards flip, rules pile up, hands fly. Slap the card the moment it fits ANY rule on the pile, but never a Hands Off card!

2–10 players (up to 4 at the table at once) · ~10–20 min · lay one phone or tablet flat in the middle

Players

Rounds

The last round is a double-points Final. Bigger groups: pick more rounds so everyone gets more turns at the table.

How to play

  1. Each player at the table gets a slap pad. On a laptop, use the keys shown on the pads.
  2. A new rule joins the pile every round. Up to 3 rules are live at once, and the oldest drops off.
  3. Slap first when the card fits any rule: +1. Slap a card that fits nothing: −1 and your pad freezes.
  4. 🚫 HANDS OFF rules beat every other rule. Slapping one of those cards counts as a wrong slap.
  5. First to 5 cards wins the round. Placings score 3 / 2 / 1 on the scoreboard, and the round winner stays at the table.
