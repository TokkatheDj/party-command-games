NEVER EVER HEADCOUNT

Never Have I Ever, with a twist: guess how many people in the room have done it, then see the real headcount!

3–10 players · about 20 minutes · one screen, one host

📋 How to play (the host runs this screen)

  1. A “Never have I ever…” card appears.
  2. Guess: everyone secretly decides how many people in the room (including themselves) have done it. Say it out loud on three — the host taps your number.
  3. Confess: the host taps everyone who really HAS done it. Honesty is the whole game!
  4. Reveal: exact guess = +2, off by one = +1. A random confessor tells the story in 20 seconds — a great story earns +1.
  5. Every 4th round is DOUBLE POINTS. Most points after the last card wins!
