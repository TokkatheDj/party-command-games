How to play

3–10 players · one phone · about 20 minutes
1️⃣
Read the goal out loudEach round opens with the round's goal and the prompt. Everyone sees it together before anyone types.
2️⃣
Answer in privateThe phone goes round the circle. Type ONE short answer — a word or two — and hit submit. Nobody else sees it.
3️⃣
Reveal togetherPut the phone where everyone can see and flip the cards. Identical answers group up automatically.
4️⃣
The room can overrule the app"HOT DOG" and "hotdog with mustard" are the same thing? Tap one card, then the other, to link them. Tap two cards already linked to split one back out.

The three goals

🦆
LONE DUCK — 3 pointsScore only if NOBODY else wrote your answer. Go obscure.
🕊️
FLOCK — 2 points eachScore 2 for every other player who wrote the same as you. Go obvious.
💞
PAIR UP — 4 pointsScore only if EXACTLY one other player matched you. Read the room.
✨
Final round is doubleEverything scores twice on the last round, so nobody is ever out of it.
