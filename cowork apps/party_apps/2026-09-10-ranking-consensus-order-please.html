HOW TO PLAY

  1. Each round, one player is the ORACLE. Everyone sees the same card: a question and five things.
  2. The Oracle takes the screen alone and secretly ranks the five in their own personal order. Then it hides.
  3. The Oracle goes silent. Everyone else argues out loud and locks in ONE ranking they think matches the Oracle's.
  4. Before the reveal, each guesser calls a Slot Stake — the one position they'd bet money on.
  5. Reveal! Every slot in exactly the right place is +3. Off by one is +1. A perfect five is +5 more.
  6. That round score goes to everyone. Correct Slot Stakes add +2 each. The Oracle can't stake, but earns +2 Poker Face if the group read them well (3+ exact).
  7. Oracle rotates. Highest total after the last round wins.

Oracle rules of honour: no hints, no faces, no "ooh". You may smile. You may not help. Being readable is worth points, so ranking honestly beats ranking weirdly.
