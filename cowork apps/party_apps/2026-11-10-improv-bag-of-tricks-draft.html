Bag of Tricks

Draft three gadgets, then talk your way out of a disaster you didn't see coming.

👥 3–8 players⏱️ 15–25 min📺 one shared screen

How to play

  1. Draft: take turns tapping a gadget from the pool until everyone has 3. A Forecast hints at the dangers ahead.
  2. Mishap: a secret disaster unfolds in 3 beats. On each beat, every player spends one gadget and says one sentence about how it saves them.
  3. Score: a gadget that fits the beat's danger is a Perfect Fit (+1). The room picks the Best Move of each beat (+2). The final round counts double.

Whoever holds the screen taps for everyone, so pass it around or let one host run it.
