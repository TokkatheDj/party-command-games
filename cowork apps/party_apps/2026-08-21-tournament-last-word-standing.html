Knockout duels, two players at a time.
Answer before the clock dies. Survive the bracket.

Who's playing? (3–10)

How to play

  • Lay the phone flat between the two duellists. Each half of the screen faces one of you — the top is upside down on purpose.
  • A challenge card flips. Most of them depend on your opponent: rhyme their word, start with its last letter, beat its length.
  • Say your answer out loud, then slap your big button to pass the turn. The timer resets shorter every single turn.
  • Clock hits zero on your turn → you lose the round.
  • Think what they just said doesn’t count? On your turn, tap ✋ THEIR ANSWER DIDN’T COUNT instead of answering — the room votes on the spot. If the room says it counted, your next turn is 2 seconds shorter. Challenge cheaply at your peril.
  • Early rounds are one round, winner takes it. Semifinal and final are first to 2.
