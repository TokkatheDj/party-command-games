BEAT CIRCLE

Count in 1 – 2 – 3 – GO! and drop a fresh answer right on the beat. Flub it, repeat it, or freeze, and you're out. The beat keeps getting faster!
3–10 players15–20 minone screen, everyone sits in a circle

How to play

1. Card Each round shows a category, a rhyme word, or a letter challenge.
2. Beat The beat counts 1-2-3-GO. Whoever is glowing answers on GO.
3. Judge Everyone is the judge! Slam MISS for a repeat, a flub, or a stall.
4. Survive +1 per answer, +3 for the last one standing. Most points wins.
