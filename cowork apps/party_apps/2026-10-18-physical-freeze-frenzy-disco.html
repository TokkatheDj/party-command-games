FREEZE FRENZY DISCO

Dance like crazy while the beat plays. When the music cuts — FREEZE in the pose on the card! Wobble and you're out of the round. Watch out for fake-outs!

3–10 players • about 15 minutes • needs a little room to wiggle • one host runs the screen

Add at least 3 players.

Rounds: 6

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Host runs the screen (that's whoever is holding the laptop or phone).
  2. A Dance Style card appears. Hit the button — music starts. Everyone dances that style!
  3. At a secret random moment the music CUTS. FREEZE in the Pose card and hold until the timer ends.
  4. Fake-out! Sometimes the music stutters for a second and comes back. Freeze during a fake-out and you're out!
  5. Host taps anyone who wobbled, laughed, moved, or froze at a fake-out. Everyone else scores +1.
  6. Host also picks the Best Pose for a +2 bonus. Most points after the last round wins!
Dance Style

Host: players get on their feet first, then tap the button.
