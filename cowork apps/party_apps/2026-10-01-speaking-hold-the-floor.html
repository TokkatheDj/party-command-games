Hold the Floor

Talk for a minute. No hesitation, no repetition, no deviation.
Get caught and someone steals your topic.

How to play ▸
  1. Put the phone in the middle. A topic appears and one player starts talking about it. Tap Start the clock the moment they begin.
  2. Anyone listening can shout "CHALLENGE!" and slam the red button if the speaker hesitates (um, er, long pause), repeats a word (words in the topic are free) or deviates from the topic. The clock freezes.
  3. The room rules. Upheld: the challenger scores 1 and takes over the same topic, and the clock runs on. Rejected: the speaker scores 1 and keeps going.
  4. Whoever is talking when the whistle blows scores 2. Talk the whole clock with no upheld challenge and get +1 bonus.
  5. Twist cards bend some topics. The final topic is worth double. Highest score wins.

Players

3 to 10 players.
