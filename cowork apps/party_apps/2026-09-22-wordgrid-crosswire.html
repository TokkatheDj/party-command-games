HOW TO PLAY

Split into two teams facing one phone. Each round is a case — first team to win 2 cases takes the match.

THE GRID

20 words sit face up. Hidden underneath:

  • 5 wires belong to Team One
  • 5 wires belong to Team Two
  • 1 CROSSWIRE belongs to both teams
  • 8 dead ends belong to nobody
  • 1 LIVE WIRE — do not touch it

Each team needs 6 wires to win the case: their own 5 plus the crosswire.

YOUR TURN

One player on the team is the Handler (rotate each case). Everyone else looks away while the Handler takes the phone and studies the key.

The Handler types one clue word and a number — how many of their wires that clue points at. Then the phone goes face up on the table and the Handler says the clue out loud and then shuts up.

The team taps a tile, then taps REVEAL to commit. You get your number of guesses plus one bonus guess.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU TAP

  • Your wire — banked, keep guessing.
  • Dead end — turn over.
  • Their wire — banked for them, turn over. Ouch.
  • CROSSWIRE — it counts for you AND them, and the sparks end your turn. Everybody needs it, nobody wants to be the one who spends a guess on it. Play chicken.
  • LIVE WIRE — the case is over and the other team wins it, right there.

CLUE LAW

One word only. It can't contain — or be contained by — any word printed on the grid. The app refuses illegal clues so nobody has to argue.

THE HANDLER MAY NOT

Speak, point, nod, grimace, or breathe suggestively after the clue is given. The team is on its own.
