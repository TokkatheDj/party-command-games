TUG OF WITS

Trivia tug-of-war! Two teams, one rope. Answer faster and smarter to drag the knot into the other team's mud.

2 teams · 2–10 players · ~10–20 min
HOW TO PLAY: Each question, one puller from each team taps an answer on their own pad. Right = haul the rope 2 (3 if you were faster than the rival). Wrong = the rope slips 1 their way. Pull the knot into their mud to win the bout!
