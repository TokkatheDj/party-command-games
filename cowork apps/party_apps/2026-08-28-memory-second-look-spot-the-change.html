How to play
  1. Study. Everyone crowds round the screen and memorises the scene before the bar runs out.
  2. Blink. The screen flashes and the scene returns with exactly one change.
  3. Shout. First person to spot it calls their name — the host taps their button.
  4. Prove it. That player taps the thing that changed. Right = points, and faster is worth more. Wrong = they sit out the rest of the round and everyone else keeps hunting.
  5. Scenes get busier and changes get sneakier every round.
